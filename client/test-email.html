🔧 Setup Instructions:

  2. Add an email service (Gmail, Outlook, etc.)

    • {{to_name}}
    • {{service_name}}
    • {{amount}}
    • {{renewal_date}}
    • {{days_until_renewal}}
    • {{message_type}}
  4. Get your Public Key, Service ID, and Template ID
